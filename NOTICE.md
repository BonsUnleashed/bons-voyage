# Credits and provenance

Bons Voyage was created for Justin's modpack and is published under the
BonsUnleashed name. Its structure generator, structure layouts, loot and
advancement data, written content and naming integration were developed with
substantial assistance from Claude and OpenAI Codex. AI assistance is disclosed;
the project is not described as entirely human-written.

The project's pre-existing source declaration was MIT, with credits to Justin
and Claude. This release supplies the full MIT text and applies that declaration
consistently to the combined structure and naming code. The former "Private pack
companion" metadata was inherited during consolidation and is replaced.

Waystones and Balm are separate required projects by BlayTheNinth. Lithostitched
is a separate world-generation library. Their code and binaries are not bundled
or relicensed here. The integration uses their public APIs and resource IDs.
Minecraft blocks, textures, sounds and other game assets remain the property of
their respective owners. The MIT license does not grant rights to third-party
game assets. No Minecraft texture or sound files are shipped in this mod.

The 2026-08-25 implementation notes record that Waystones' block-entity format was
checked against Waystones and a Towns and Towers compatibility template. This
is recorded as a format reference, not a claim that those projects authored
the Bons Voyage structure catalogue. The release source includes the authored
Python generator and Java integration so the distributed content can be rebuilt.

Screenshots and captured footage show Minecraft running the mod. Any separately
provided promotional illustration has its own provenance and is not evidence of
in-game appearance. The old generated promotional cover is not included in the
public JAR or source archive.

The trailer's music is "Legionnaire (Original)" by Scott Buckley, released under the
Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International license (www.scottbuckley.com.au).
The required credit appears on the front page, on the trailer's end card and in
media/provenance.json. The track is not part of the mod or its source archive.
